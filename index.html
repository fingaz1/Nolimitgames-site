Started as a class project. Never stopped.

In 2010 a handful of students set out to build their first game, Vengeance. The team scattered years ago; the work didn't. No Limit Games is now run solo by its co-founder, who kept designing — first on the side, then in earnest — until "someday" turned into a slate of a dozen tabletop and video game projects in various stages of finish.

The studio's first release, Battle of Souls, is headed to Gamefound this month, with a deckbuilder expansion and several finished titles queued up behind it.
